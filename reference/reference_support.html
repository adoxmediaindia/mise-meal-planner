Common questions

How do I change my meal plan?

Open your current week and tap Regenerate plan, or swap individual meals from the recipe screen.

How does the shopping list work?

mise combines ingredients across your week's recipes into one list, grouped for your chosen supermarket.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open your Apple subscription settings. On Android, open Google Play subscriptions.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email support@misemealplanner.com and ask for your account and associated data to be deleted.

Allergies and dietary requirements

mise uses the preferences you set during onboarding to filter recipes. Always check ingredient labels and recipes before cooking — we cannot guarantee allergen safety.
